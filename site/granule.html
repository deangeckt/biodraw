Granule cell

Mostly soma, with four short dendrites that end in claws — the cell whose proportions are its identity.

neuro.Granule

Drawn at a basket cell's proportions it stops reading as a granule cell, so length is the knob that matters here — not the count.

Proportion is the identity

The claw on the end

Drawing one

import biodraw as bd

cell = bd.neuro.Granule(
    length=0.85,            # barely longer than the soma — the identity knob
    dendrites=4,            # how many leave
    forks=0.62,             # the claw: a late split...
    fork_angle_deg=44.0,    # ...at a wide angle
    depth=2,
)
cell.draw(ax=ax, wall_lw=0.9)
cell.fit(ax, pad=0.12)

Every figure above is output, not a stored asset — drawn by granule/build.py, in Python, on a matplotlib axes.
